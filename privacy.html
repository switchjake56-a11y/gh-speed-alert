GH Speed Alert Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Who we are

GH Speed Alert is developed by Massa Kwame (Femi). It helps drivers in Ghana see their speed and get alerts when they pass the speed limit.

Location

The app uses your phone’s GPS only while you have started it. Your speed, trip distance and top speed are worked out on your phone. The developer does not receive them.

Automatic speed limit

When Auto speed limit is on, the app sends your current coordinates (latitude and longitude) to the public OpenStreetMap Overpass servers (overpass-api.de and overpass.kumi.systems) to find the road you are on and its posted limit. Those servers have their own privacy practices. The developer does not receive or store these coordinates. To stop this, choose a fixed speed limit (30, 50, 90 or 100) instead of Auto.

Data stored on your phone

  • Your settings (alert sound, voice, allowance, speed limit choice)
  • Your journey history: date, distance, top speed and duration
  • Your vehicle info: car name and number plate, if you enter them
  • Speed cameras you save (their coordinates)
  • Black box incidents you save: the last 20 minutes of speed and route points
  • Your emergency contact name and phone number, if you enter them
  • Saved road data: places where the app already found a road’s speed limit

This stays on your phone. You can clear journey history inside the app, or remove everything by clearing the site data or uninstalling the app.

Sharing your location

The Share my location button and the Map Preview tile only work when you tap them. Share my location creates a map link that you send through an app you choose. Map Preview opens OpenStreetMap with your position.

Crash alert and SOS

If you turn on Crash alert, the app reads your phone’s motion sensor on the device to spot a hard impact. Nothing from the sensor leaves your phone. When you send an SOS, the app opens your messaging app with your location link ready, and the message is sent by you through that app.

What we do not do

There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics in the app. We do not sell your data. The app is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep ordinary server logs such as your IP address under GitHub’s own policy.

Safety

Speed limits shown by the app come from map data and estimates and can be wrong or out of date. Always follow road signs and the law. Do not handle your phone while driving.

Children

The app is for drivers and is not aimed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
