Speed Limit Detection

Auto: finding the road…

Auto finds your road on OpenStreetMap and uses its posted limit. Where none is posted it estimates from Ghana’s general limits. It can be wrong where roads aren’t mapped, so always follow road signs. Pick a number to set the limit yourself.

Stay safe
  • Wear your seat belt, and make sure passengers do too.
  • Keep a safe gap from the vehicle ahead, more in rain.
  • Don’t use your phone while driving. Set up before you move.
  • Never drive after drinking or when very tired.
  • Slow down early for towns, markets, schools and bends.

My Vehicle Info

Speed Cameras

Save a speed camera when you pass it. The app warns you when you come within 300 m of a saved camera. Saved cameras stay on this phone. Save them while parked or ask a passenger. Never use the phone while driving.

Black Box

While GPS is on, the app keeps the last 20 minutes of your speed and route on your phone. After an incident, tap Save incident to keep a copy you can download as a spreadsheet file. If Crash alert is on, one is saved for you.

About GH Speed Alert

Road Safety for a Greater Ghana. GH Speed Alert uses your phone’s GPS to show your speed and warns you when you go over the speed limit.
