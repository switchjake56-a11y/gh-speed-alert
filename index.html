Road Safety Tips

  • Follow the posted limit. It is set for the road, not for your car.
  • Slow down near schools, markets and pedestrian crossings.
  • Everyone wears a seat belt, front and back.
  • Keep your phone down while driving.
  • Leave a safe gap. In rain and at night, slow down more.
  • Never drive tired or after drinking.
  • Riders: always wear a helmet.
  • Do not overtake on bends, hills or near junctions.
